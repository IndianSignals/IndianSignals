How to read this table
ACTIVE = signal is live. It does not mean the trade is in profit.
  ⏳ Waiting = price has not reached the entry yet. The entry window is still open (3 candles of the signal timeframe, e.g. 3 hours for 1H). The trade has not started.
  ▶ Running = entry is filled. The trade now runs until TP or SL.
TP1 / TP2 HIT = price touched that target after entering. The trade is still running toward the next target.
TP3 HIT = final target reached, trade finished in profit.
SL HIT = stop loss touched, trade finished at a loss. If SL and a target touch in the same candle, SL is counted first.
EXPIRED = price never entered the entry zone within the entry window (no trade taken, not a win or loss). Do not enter late; wait for a fresh signal.
One at a time = a pair gets a new signal only after its current signal is complete (TP3 hit, SL hit or expired).
Change % = live price vs entry. Green = in profit, red = in loss (LONG: price up = +, SHORT: price down = +).
Direction = LONG (buy) / SHORT (sell). R:R = reward to risk using TP1.
TP / SL hits are auto-tracked from exchange candles (wick touch counts as a hit), so results are an estimate, not real fills. Not financial advice, always manage your own risk.
